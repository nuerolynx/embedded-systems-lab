# Revision history

## A0 preliminary engineering release — 2026-09-26

- Six-layer, 36 × 124 mm carrier with a 12 × 10 mm lower cable notch; nominal 1.6 mm thickness.
- 9–16 V input; ESP32-WROOM-32E, ID-12LA-HE and PN5321 MINI V2.
- Routed schematic/PCB, portable libraries/models, BOM, previews, enclosure source/meshes and separate relay demonstration board.
- Final ERC and DRC clean, all nets connected, schematic/PCB parity clean; four firmware builds compiled.
- Physical fit, RF coexistence, exact PDK support, protection performance and panel interoperability remain unvalidated. Not released for fabrication.

## A1 two-variant revision — requested, not implemented

The requested 12/24 V pigtail and Ethernet/PoE variants, 116 × 43 × 22 mm enclosure, center-rear cable exit, ToF intent sensor and ten-segment light bar are defined in [the roadmap](docs/two-variant-roadmap.md). A0 files and compiled results do not validate those changes.
