# Bring-up and acceptance plan

This sequence has not been performed on hardware. Compilation and electrical-rule checks do not establish physical or panel compatibility.

1. Review the final DRC report, unresolved connections, footprint pin mapping and assembly placement. Resolve shorts, unrouted circuits, RF keepout violations and fit blockers before fabrication. Print the LF land pattern at 1:1 scale and compare it with the actual module. Check every connection of the supplied seven-pin HF harness. Review [mechanical fit](../mechanical/mechanical-fit.md), including LF lead trimming, roof pockets, the rear wall mounting slot and cable routing.
2. With modules removed where practical, check for unintended shorts among VIN, GND, 3.3 V and 5 V, and verify diode/transistor orientation. Apply 9 V with current limiting; check the rails before testing 12 V, 16 V, voltage ramps, brownouts and reversed input. Record current, ripple and startup overshoot.
3. Hold `ESP_EN` low and confirm that the Wiegand sinks, RGB drivers, buzzer and relay output remain inactive. Measure Q6 base net `BUZZ_BASE` during reset: R24 is the 2.2 kΩ base-to-ground pull-down, reached from GPIO14 through R23. Measure Wiegand leakage with panel pull-ups no higher than 15 V and current limited to 5 mA per line. Follow [tamper assembly](tamper-assembly.md): the selected Y31B13136FPLFG / KSC223G LFG SPST-NO switch is held pressed when assembled and has a remote 1 kΩ series resistor. With power off and the remote loop disconnected, verify approximately 1 kΩ only while pressed and an open circuit when released. Under power, confirm LOW assembled and HIGH released or with either wire cut. Verify plunger travel and hard-stop behavior; carrier and actuator fit remain unresolved.
4. Program through a UART fixture with 3.3 V logic. Hold `BOOT_N` low, pulse `ESP_EN` low and release it, then release `BOOT_N` after entry into the bootloader. Never inject 5 V into a service GPIO or power the reader from J2 pad 6. Run credential-independent output and loop-timing diagnostics before credential tests.
5. Capture LF UART traffic at 9600 baud, 8-N-1, using identified EM and HID credentials and the exact PDK wristband model. Confirm idle-high logic on module pin 9. Archive complete byte/bit captures. Resolve the vendor's nibble-order and frame-description ambiguities before enabling any HID-to-26-bit conversion.
6. Confirm PN5321 MINI hardware V2, the SPI solder selector and reset wiring. Read complete 4-, 7- and 10-byte ISO/IEC 14443A UIDs where the available test credentials support those lengths. Capture SPI ready status, ACKs and recovery from an absent card, a disconnected cable and module reset.
7. Follow [RF tuning](rf-tuning.md): test each RF section separately, then together. Evaluate orientation, mutual detuning, ferrite, fasteners, the metal mullion and the closed plastic enclosure. Include the front TVS row near the HF antenna. Record field measurements, success rates and latency distributions. Automatic LF/HF interleaving would require a hardware change adding MCU control of LF reset; Rev A provides only the manual JP3 reset connection.
8. Provision a unique Secure Channel base key (SCBK) with the wired `provision` image, then flash the normal `opendual` image while preserving NVS. Verify connection with the trusted key, refusal with an incorrect or missing key, reboot behavior and interrupted authenticated `KEYSET` persistence. Check the Control Panel's interpretation of every credential kind and UID length. The reader operates as an OSDP Peripheral Device (PD).
9. Measure RS-485 direction timing, polarity, cable-end termination and panel bias. Wiegand credential transmission is disabled by the default raw-length policy; configure an explicitly supported HF/EM raw mapping or use controlled output diagnostics before measuring the intended 50 µs sink pulses, 2 ms bit slots and loaded LOW voltage. HID-to-26-bit conversion remains blocked pending capture evidence. Test the actual target panel: a frame with a valid CRC does not establish that the panel accepts the credential.
10. For `STANDALONE_DEMO`, use a separate relay driver, coil supply and switched-load supply. Measure the intended 3 s relay cutoff, behavior after reset or brownout, and cancellation when tamper is asserted. Test coil suppression and load release time independently with the actual relay and load. The empty-by-default UID allowlist is a demonstration, not cryptographic authentication.
11. Perform thermal soak, input transient, ESD, EMC and environmental tests against an agreed product standard and test plan. Record conditions, equipment and raw results. No certification or physical test result is claimed by this project.
