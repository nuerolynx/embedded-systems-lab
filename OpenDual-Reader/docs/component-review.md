# Component and interface review

Reviewed 2026-09-26 against `hardware/design-data.json` and the generator source. This is a bounded, independent pinout, rating and ordering-code review. It is not a complete procurement qualification, surge test, worst-case analysis, or layout sign-off. Generator corrections must be reconciled with the native schematic, PCB and validation reports while preserving the final routing; regenerating the initial board would overwrite later layout work. The status table below records the inspected artifact, not an assumption that an intended edit was applied.

## Pins and electrical bounds checked

**Power.** Traco TSR1-2433 and TSR1-2450 use pins 1 input, 2 ground, 3 output. Both are 1A parts. The 3.3 V model accepts 4.75–36 V, the 5 V model 6.5–36 V. A 9–16 V input after the series diode is compatible at nominal conditions. Thermal derating above 60 °C, actual load transients, ripple and startup must be measured. The 11.7×7.5×10.1 mm nominal case carries ±0.5 mm general tolerance, so reserve 10.6 mm height. The manufacturer's PDF text was accessed through its search index; its drawing was not visually inspected. [Traco datasheet](https://www.tracopower.com/tsr1-datasheet).

**ESP32.** The selected WROOM-32E-N4 pin assignments match the manufacturer's module. Pins 17–22 and 32 are NC on this module and should stay unconnected; do not substitute pin functions from an older WROOM symbol. GPIO34 and GPIO36 are input-only and require the shown external input sources/pull-ups. GPIO0 is the boot strap. Module power sequencing, RF keepout and EN requirements remain subject to board review. [Espressif module datasheet](https://documentation.espressif.com/esp32-wroom-32e_esp32-wroom-32ue_datasheet_en.html).

**Reset behavior.** GPIO14/MTMS reset state has pull-up enabled. With a typical 45 kΩ internal pull-up, a 2.2 kΩ series base resistor and 2.2 kΩ base pulldown give an approximate inactive base voltage of 0.15 V; that estimate is not a guaranteed worst-case internal-resistor limit. When driven high, using VOH=2.64 V and VBE=0.85 V gives approximately (2.64−0.85)/2200−0.85/2200=0.427 mA available base current, sufficient as a preliminary choice for a roughly 5 mA load. Bench check silence before initialization and during brownout. [ESP32 technical reference manual, GPIO reset table](https://documentation.espressif.com/esp32_technical_reference_manual_en.pdf).

**RS-485.** THVD2410DR is the same SOIC8 electrical mapping: 1 R, 2 /RE, 3 DE, 4 D, 5 GND, 6 A, 7 B, 8 supply. It supports 3.3 V supply and CMOS receive output, 500 kbps and ±70 V bus fault limits. SM712 pins 1/2 are the lines and 3 is ground. Its positive maximum clamp is 31 V at the rated 19A 8/20µs pulse. This coordinates more plausibly than the original ±18 V part, but lead inductance, ground return and actual transient source remain untested. Avoid adding arbitrary 10 Ω series resistors per line: they reduce differential drive and must be included in the terminated-bus calculation. [TI THVD2410](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/thvd2410.pdf), [Littelfuse SM712](https://www.littelfuse.com/assetdocs/tvs-diode-array-spa-sm712-datasheet?assetguid=8313a28c-8802-4d47-a2a7-e30b5b1f67d8).

**Wiegand and UI transistors.** MMBT3904LT1G SOT23 pins are 1 base, 2 emitter, 3 collector. The 40 V collector rating is not a permitted panel supply rating. At the intended 5 mA maximum panel sink current, each 47 Ω series resistor adds 0.235 V. Adding approximately 0.2 V transistor saturation gives approximately 0.435 V at the panel wire; confirm the actual panel threshold, cable current, temperature and pulse shape. The 2.2 kΩ base drive is reasonable for this restricted load. Do not advertise unrestricted open-collector current. [onsemi datasheet](https://www.onsemi.com/pdf/datasheet/mmbt3904lt1-d.pdf).

For unidirectional panel data lines, a unidirectional TVS is preferred so negative transients use the TVS's forward characteristic instead of its negative avalanche voltage. This is an engineering inference about the shown circuit, not a tested system rating. The selected manufacturer is **Littelfuse**: **SMBJ18A** for D2 and **SMBJ15A** for D4/D5. These are manufacturer part numbers, not shorthand requiring an invented package suffix. The [Littelfuse SMBJ family](https://m.littelfuse.com/~/media/electronics/datasheets/tvs_diodes/littelfuse_tvs_diode_smbj_datasheet.pdf.pdf) lists SMBJ15A at 15 V stand-off and 24.4 V rated positive clamp, and SMBJ18A at 18 V stand-off and 29.2 V clamp. Its manufacturer PDF was available as indexed text but the direct URL redirected. Obtain a current, readable Littelfuse sheet and inspect package polarity and full pulse curves before procurement; do not treat another manufacturer's same base designation as a qualified substitute.

**RGB.** Würth 150141M173100: pin 1 common anode, pin 2 blue cathode, pin 3 red cathode, pin 4 green cathode. The assigned exact-part KiCad footprint mapping agrees with the visually checked manufacturer top view. 5 V anode and transistor sinks avoid trying to drive the blue/green LEDs directly from a 3.3 V output. 330 Ω red and 220 Ω green/blue are plausible prototype values; visual balance and thermal/current measurements remain open. [Würth datasheet](https://www.we-online.com/components/products/datasheet/150141M173100.pdf).

**Buzzer.** PKMCS0909E4000-R1 is an externally driven piezo, not a DC buzzer or inductive coil. Use approximately 4 kHz drive. The 5 V unipolar square-wave amplitude is below the stated ±12.5Vo-p maximum; the 1 kΩ shunt/discharge path draws approximately 5 mA and 25 mW when pulled low. Verify edges, DC bias, actual drive waveform and acoustic performance in the enclosure. The supplier's SPL figure uses different excitation and cannot be claimed for this product. [Murata product note](https://www.murata.com/~/media/webrenewal/products/sound/sounder/vppt-buzj083-d.ashx).

**LF and HF.** The LF processor UART is pin 9 per the manufacturer's processor-connection section; pin 8 is pseudo-RS232. Both module supply and ESP32 input are 3.3 V in this carrier. The LF drawing and custom footprint are documented separately. LF pins 3/4 remain unconnected because this is the internally tuned antenna model. The purchased HF module uses 3.3 V host logic while accepting the selected 5 V supply. See `rf-tuning.md` and `datasheet-references.md` for exact module and harness mappings.

**Tamper contact.** The selected remote assembly now uses Littelfuse/C&K **Y31B13136FPLFG / KSC223G LFG**, a gold SPST normally open switch, with a remote 1 kΩ series resistor **RC0603FR-071KL**. It must be held pressed by a supported actuator while assembled: pressing closes the loop, and release or disconnection asserts tamper. The actuator linkage has not been designed or verified. The manufacturer gold-contact minimum is 0.1 mA and 20 mV. The series resistor limits C13 discharge; nominal closed current is 0.30 mA and GPIO36 LOW is about 0.30 V. These are calculated values, not measurements. The earlier D2F-01L proposal was replaced because its visually inspected microload graph begins at 5 V. No main-board GPIO, R26, C13 or J4 net change is required. Remote carrier design, terminal-pair verification, plunger preload, overtravel and physical testing remain open. See [tamper assembly](tamper-assembly.md) and the [current C&K KSC2 datasheet](https://www.ckswitches.com/media/1968/ksc2.pdf).

## BOM, assembly metadata and model provenance audit

The main BOM's populated electrical references were compared with `hardware/design-data.json`. The RF1 purchased HF assembly, SW1 remote tamper switch and RT1 remote resistor are off-board additions; J3 and J4 are their carrier solder lands, not duplicate purchased assemblies. J1–J5 and JP1–JP4 have no commercial mating-connector MPN: their BOM descriptions deliberately identify PCB lands or service shorts. A harness, strain relief and programming fixture still require assembly specifications. Placement coordinates in design data are initial proposals; the final native PCB is the placement authority.

The exact-part manufacturer selections are ID Innovations (U4), ELECHOUSE (RF1), Espressif (U3), TRACO Power (U1/U2), Texas Instruments (U5), onsemi (Q1–Q6), Würth Elektronik (D6), Murata (capacitors and BZ1), Littelfuse (F1/D2–D5), Diodes Incorporated (D1), Vishay (R7), Yageo (other resistors), and Littelfuse/C&K (SW1); the remote series resistor RT1 is Yageo. The BOM is a prototype selection list, not a stock, lifecycle or second-source qualification.

The copied standard KiCad STEP assets are library package representations. Even an exact-looking filename does not establish current manufacturer CAD or a complete maximum-material/tolerance model. The LF, HF board, HF antenna and fuse WRL files are explicitly dimensional proxies. `model-validation.json` checks that referenced model files exist; its zero missing-file count does **not** verify dimensions, polarity, fit or manufacturability. Remote wiring, harness connector envelopes and tamper actuator travel need separate physical checks. The project's library-provenance note records the upstream library and license source. No component model is evidence that a physical prototype was assembled or tested.

## Still open before buying or fabricating

- Fully qualify the exact MLCC suffixes, effective capacitance under DC bias, tolerance, aging, availability and height. C1 GRM32ER71H106KA12L is confirmed as 10 µF, 50 V, X7R, 1210 by the [Murata-authored sheet mirrored by RS](https://docs.rs-online.com/2a0d/0900766b813885a3.pdf); height is 2.5±0.2 mm. Full current producer sheets for the other exact capacitor suffixes were not retrieved in this review. Manufacturer-page failures alone do not prove those parts invalid.
- Confirm the specified Littelfuse SMBJ18A/SMBJ15A purchase matches the current package drawing, polarity and pulse curves. Same-number devices from other suppliers are not automatically qualified substitutes.
- Check PTC hold current at maximum enclosure temperature, fault-trip coordination, cable power budget and startup inrush; a nominal 0.75A marking is insufficient by itself.
- Verify all purchased physical parts against the final footprint and assembly drawing, especially provisional LF pin tolerances, HF harness orientation, module height, lead trim and remote tamper-switch travel, preload and series-resistor assembly.
- Complete board electrical checks, RF coexistence/range tests, panel interoperability, transient tests and thermal measurements. This review does not convert an unrouted or untested design into a fabrication release.
